Generate Your Bilingual NDA — Free

When two companies from different countries negotiate a deal, a single-language NDA often leaves one side reading a document that isn't in their working language — a real source of misunderstandings and disputes down the line. DraftB2B's bilingual NDA generator solves this by producing a genuine dual-language document: your confidentiality agreement rendered in two languages side by side in the same PDF, clause by clause, so both parties can read and sign a version they fully understand without needing a separate certified translation.

This template covers the essentials of a mutual (bilateral) NDA — disclosure and use of confidential information, exclusions, duration, remedies, and governing law — adapted to the standard structure used in international B2B negotiations. Because DraftB2B runs entirely in your browser, none of the information you enter (company names, addresses, tax IDs, or the confidential subject matter itself) is ever transmitted to or stored on a server. The document, its PDF/Word export, and its SHA-256 integrity hash are all generated locally on your device.

How it works — 3 steps

  1. Choose your languages. Open the generator with bilingual mode already set to English/Spanish, or pick any of the 8 supported languages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a bilingual NDA legally valid in both languages?+

A bilingual NDA generated side-by-side in two languages is a common and accepted format for cross-border agreements, as long as both parties agree on which language version governs in case of a discrepancy — DraftB2B lets you state that explicitly in the jurisdiction clause. It does not replace review by a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction.

Which languages can I generate the NDA in?+

DraftB2B supports 8 languages — Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, and Hindi — and can pair any two of them side by side in bilingual mode.

Is my data sent to a server?+

No. DraftB2B is 100% client-side: the document, the PDF, the Word file, and the SHA-256 integrity seal are all generated inside your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to or stored on any backend.

Do I need to create an account?+
